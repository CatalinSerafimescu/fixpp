## The paired loader bench (SC-004, `[const §VIII.2]`)

- [ ] CHK001 SC-004 times only `xml_loader_bench`, which measures `XmlLoader::load`. The record growth and the widened identity also reach `OrchestraLoader::load` and the overlay merge. Is a paired row required for each of them, or is its absence stated with a reason? [Coverage, Spec §SC-004, research D6]
- [ ] CHK002 Research D6 says the bench's fixed `monotonic_buffer_resource` falls back to its upstream on FIX50SP2, and that larger records push more of the load onto that fallback. Is it stated that the bench's allocator matches a production load's? The C ABI loads with `std::pmr::new_delete_resource()`. If they differ, the +5% does not measure the change a user sees. [Assumption, research D6]
- [ ] CHK003 Is the paired run's noise band stated, with its repetitions, A-B-A-B rounds and min-per-tree, so that a +5% result can be told from noise? Is T060's seeded delay sized against that band? [Measurability, Spec §SC-004, tasks T060, `[const §VIII.2]`]
- [ ] CHK004 SC-004 says "the deciding run is the one after that rebase". Is the outcome stated for a run before the rebase that exceeds +5%? It could decide nothing, or it could stop the work. [Clarity, Spec §SC-004, plan "Constitution Check" row VIII §2]
- [ ] CHK005 SC-004 says "or carries the §2 approval". Does it also state that the approver of a slowdown above +5% is someone other than the author, as `[const §VIII.2]` requires and research D6 repeats? [Consistency, Spec §SC-004, research D6, `[const §VIII.2]`]
- [ ] CHK006 Is it stated which dictionaries the paired bench loads (FIX50SP2 only, or every vendored base), and that the base with the most components is among them? [Clarity, Spec §SC-004, research D6]

## Footprint and memory

- [ ] CHK007 SC-004 records `table_view_footprint_bench` bytes for the base and the head. Research Survey says `table_view`'s stores hold tags, not record indexes, and the records that grow (`ComponentRef`, `GroupRef`) live in the dictionary's metadata. Can that instrument see the growth it is recorded for, or does SC-004 need another measure, such as the metadata handle's record bytes? [Measurability, Spec §SC-004, research D6 "Footprint", research Survey "Public"]
- [ ] CHK008 The footprint is "recorded, not timed" and has no budget. Is it stated that SC-004's footprint half cannot fail? If it can, is the growth that would block stated? [Clarity, Spec §SC-004]
- [ ] CHK009 Is a loaded dictionary's memory growth stated as a derivation rather than a measured number, so it stays true as dictionaries change? - [ ] CHK010 Is an Article VIII memory budget for a loaded dictionary named and the growth checked against it? If none applies, is that stated? [Gap, `[const §VIII.2]`, research D6]
