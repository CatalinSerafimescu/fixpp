## Phase 3: User Story 1 - Restarted session resumes its OUTBOUND numbering from disk (Priority: P1) 🎯 MVP

**Goal**: On cold open over a persistent store, the in-memory next-outbound is loaded from the store **before** any outbound seqnum is sampled, so the first outbound message (e.g. the initiator Logon) carries `34 = N` (the persisted value), not `34 = 1`; hydrate is one-shot/cold (never re-hydrates on reconnect), latched-after-success, and `reset_on_logon` still wins. (FR-003/004/008, SC-001, D-1/D-6/D-9, INV-H3/H5/H6)

**Independent Test**: pre-seed a persistent store to `next_outbound = 42`, construct a fresh initiator over it, open, drive the Logon ⇒ the Logon carries `34 = 42` (not `34 = 1`). (SC-001)
