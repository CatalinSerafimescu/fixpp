# Concurrency Checklist: FIXT application version, the validation half (S-026)

**Purpose**: Requirements-quality review, for the Gate B reviewer, of the threading half of 099. In scope: FR-027, FR-028 (`start()`'s failure), NFR-003's threading half, plan's `[const §XI.7]`, `[const §XIII.3]`, `[const §XV.9]` rows and the owner's information list, data-model E-1, E-4's scratch argument, E-6, research R-3, surface.md's `Engine::start()` row and Python's `engine_start` row, and contracts W-15, W-20. The route-set design and the blocking `start()` were signed off under `[const §X.6]`. An item here asks whether a consequence is stated, never whether the decision should change.

## `Engine::start()` blocking

- [ ] CHK014 Surface.md states that `start()` blocks and that a host on `exec` stalls that thread. Is it stated whether such a call is supported, advised against or a precondition violation? Research R-3 says "its precondition changes" without saying what it changes to. [Clarity, contracts surface.md, research R-3]
- [ ] CHK015 Plan lists the blocking `start()` "for the owner's information" and surface.md lists it as a C++ contract. Quickstart's L-rows and B-rows and T127 and T128 do not. Is this shipped behaviour a user must know recorded in the B&L file? [Completeness, plan owner's information list, quickstart.md, tasks T127, tasks T128]
- [ ] CHK016 `engine.hpp`'s lifecycle says "`start()` once", research R-3 says "legal once", and `engine.h` says a second call returns a domain error. W-20's Engine arm requires a second `start()` to succeed after `out_of_memory`. Is that exception stated in the contract, at both the C++ and C levels? [Conflict, contracts W-20, research R-3, Spec §FR-028]
- [ ] CHK017 Is the state after a failed `start()` stated in lifecycle terms: still `constructed`, nothing spawned, no set held? E-1 says "nothing spawned". [Completeness, data-model E-1, Spec §FR-028]
- [ ] CHK018 During the walks, is `stop()` from another thread specified, along with the any-thread readers `lookup()` and `acceptor_bound_endpoint()`? `engine.hpp` calls `stop()` idempotent from any state, and the set's build cannot be cancelled. [Gap, research R-3, contracts surface.md]
- [ ] CHK019 Research R-3 says a stall on `exec` affects "every strand on that thread". Is the consequence for another engine's sessions stated: a heartbeat or Logon timer that fires late across the stall? [Edge Case, research R-3, contracts surface.md]
- [ ] CHK020 Python's `engine_start` holds the GIL across the walks, and research R-11 says a second engine's worker then waits in `PyGILState_Ensure`. Is that wait stated as a consequence for that engine's sessions, and recorded beyond the GIL table's row? [Completeness, research R-11, contracts surface.md (Python)]
- [ ] CHK021 Ruling 1 says validation-off sessions are unchanged. A non-validating FIXT session's engine also walks every table and blocks in `start()`, which FR-011 does not list among what such sessions gain. Is that cost stated for them? [Consistency, Spec ruling 1, Spec §FR-011, data-model E-1, Spec §NFR-003]
