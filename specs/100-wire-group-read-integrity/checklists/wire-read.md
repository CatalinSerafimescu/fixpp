## The probe budget (FR-008, research D1)

- [ ] CHK001 Is "occurrences" in FR-008 the same quantity as D1's `entries_.size()`, so that repeated group-member tags count toward the budget, and does that agree with FR-008's "a build within it indexes every distinct tag"? [Clarity, Spec §FR-008, research D1]
- [ ] CHK002 Is one probe defined? Does inspecting the home slot count, and does an insert at its home slot cost one probe or zero? Without that, the budget's boundary cannot be stated exactly for a frame at the edge. [Clarity, research D1]
- [ ] CHK003 Is the budget stated, in one place, for every build that runs `OffsetTable::build`: root tables, nested sub-tables (D7 relies on it) and dict-free tables (Edge Cases)? Do FR-008 and research D7 agree on where a sub-table that exceeds it ends up (`limit_exceeded` on the read, not a parse failure)? [Consistency, Spec §FR-008, Spec §FR-011, research D7, Spec "Edge Cases"]
- [ ] CHK004 D1's measurement says "no trial's total probe work came near the per-build budget". Is "came near" given a margin, or a recipe whose output the verify record carries, so that the claim that non-crafted frames stay within the budget can be re-checked? [Measurability, research D1 "Measurement"]
- [ ] CHK005 Is it a stated precondition of US3.1 that the crafted frame stays inside the entry limit and the inbound byte limit, so that a frame failing on the entry cap or on framing is not accepted as the budget's witness? [Clarity, Spec §US3.1, research D9 "#645"]
- [ ] CHK006 US3.2's within-budget frame that reaches today's per-insert cap comes from a seed search (tasks T007 (c)). Does the spec's acceptance text allow for the frame's existence depending on that search, and does it state the ending when no seed is found within the bound? T007 says the setup then fails. [Edge Case, Spec §US3.2, tasks T007]
- [ ] CHK007 Is it stated as a requirement with a witness, and not only as a survey fact, that `find()` still finds a tag placed more than `kMaxBuildProbe` slots from its home slot, because its walk is bounded by `overlay_.size()`? [Coverage, research D1, research "Survey"]
- [ ] CHK008 The ending reuses `wire_offset_table_full`, the entry-cap error. Is it stated whether a caller or a log can tell the two causes apart, and that this is intended (D1 "Alternatives" rejects a new slot)? [Clarity, Spec "Clarifications", research D1 "Alternatives"]
- [ ] CHK009 Is the terminal close stated as the ending for every resend of the same crafted frame while the process seed lives, given that the seed is per process and the production seed is not weakened? [Coverage, Spec §US3.3, research D1 "Rationale", Spec "Assumptions"]

## Group-ness: the read order (FR-001 to FR-003, research D2)

- [ ] CHK010 Edge Cases says that a declared count of `0` followed by member fields "follows the declared count, as today". Research D2 says the count's value is never read, and its step 6 runs the extent walk by membership. Do the two agree on how many instances such a frame reads (0, or the instances found)? Is that ending stated on both getters and on the typed path? [Conflict, Spec "Edge Cases", research D2, Spec §FR-002]
- [ ] CHK011 Is the shape in CHK010 (declared `0`, member fields after it) witnessed by a named cell, or is it left out of research D9 and tasks T010 on purpose? [Coverage, Spec "Edge Cases", research D9]
- [ ] CHK012 D2 step 3 answers a tag that is not registered with `err_required_field_missing`, "as today's absent answer". Is it stated how a caller of `group()` other than the getters tells absent from not-a-group, given that `is_group` exists only on the result structs? [Clarity, research D2, research D3]
- [ ] CHK013 Is "the read's context" defined for each read path: the root table `(msg_type, [])`, a sub-table's stored context, and the six-argument `nested_group_slices` with a context the caller supplies? [Clarity, Spec §FR-001, research D5 "Reachability", research D11]
- [ ] CHK014 Does a dict-aware bundle with a null registration callback (Clarifications) get the same ending as a dict-free table on both getters and on the typed path? Is that ending written into the contract's table or its "Unchanged" list? [Consistency, Spec "Clarifications", data-model "dict_hooks", contract "Unchanged"]
- [ ] CHK015 Now that the value never decides group-ness, are endings stated for a registered count field whose value is not a number (`""`, `abc`, `-1`), which `parse_bounded_u32` reads as 0 (research D2 "Alternatives")? Is it stated that such a field reads as a group by membership alone, like a lying count? [Edge Case, Gap, research D2]

## Zero instances and the typed view (FR-002, US1)

- [ ] CHK016 US1.6 says the typed result "has size 0 and is not failed". The Assumptions say that present-with-zero and absent both read as size 0 there. Is the missing typed presence notion stated as a known difference from the C ABI, which tells `OK`/0 from `TAG_NOT_FOUND`? [Consistency, Spec §US1.6, Spec "Assumptions"]
- [ ] CHK017 Does the spec itself state the typed ending for a tag that is not registered (US1.8's 802 at the root, US1.9's 453): size 0, not failed? Today it is stated only in the contract's typed table and in tasks T016. [Completeness, contract "Typed C++", Spec §US1.8, Spec §US1.9]
- [ ] CHK018 FR-002 says "wherever the count field sits". Does that cover a count field that is the last entry of a sub-table built over an instance slice? Is the shortcut's place in D2's order (after registration) stated as the only rule that separates that field from a scalar that is last in its instance? [Clarity, Spec §FR-002, research D2, Spec "Edge Cases"]

## Read status, caches and failures (FR-005, FR-007, FR-011, research D5, D7, D10, D11)

- [ ] CHK019 FR-007 says "a completed read's repeat fails only where the getter allocates a cursor shell". Edge Cases says a first and a repeat read "both return the same code", and US1.11 says "the second answer is the first one". Are these scoped so that an n ≥ 1 read whose repeat fails at the shell does not break the other two? [Consistency, Spec §FR-007, Spec "Edge Cases", Spec §US1.11]
- [ ] CHK020 D10 relies on a monotonic arena ("on a monotonic resource every repeat fails the same way"). MSVC debug forwards `arena_upstream()` to the heap. Is the repeat-equals-first requirement (US2.6, FR-007) stated as conditional on a monotonic arena? [Assumption, research D10, Spec §US2.6, research D9 "Lanes"]
- [ ] CHK021 Is "present in the instance" (D11's presence scan, and contract row 1 for the nested getter) defined as present at the instance's own level, or anywhere in the slice, deeper nested instances included? A tag that is present only inside a nested instance would read `TYPE_MISMATCH` or `WIRE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` instead of `TAG_NOT_FOUND`. [Ambiguity, research D11, contract "Return codes"]
- [ ] CHK022 Is the result of D11's allocation-free scan stated for a slice whose Length+Data split through the caller's `hooks.data_tag_for_length` differs from the root's split (the caller-bundle case D11 names with fixpp#426)? [Edge Case, research D11]
- [ ] CHK023 Is a precedence between `limit_exceeded` and `alloc_failed` stated for a read where both could apply? Examples: a sub-build that exceeds its budget after an allocation has already failed, or a cache push that fails on a read that would hit `wire_group_too_large`. [Clarity, data-model "group_read_status", research D10]
- [ ] CHK024 Is D5's top-level reachability ("where the root's entry cap exceeds the per-instance cap") stated for the shipped session configuration? It should show whether FR-011's top-level arm is reachable on a validation-off session, and not only through a test-built `MessageView`. [Clarity, research D5 "Reachability", Spec §FR-011]
- [ ] CHK025 FR-011 calls a nested `wire_group_too_large` defensive. Contract row 3 has a malformed-data arm that is "determined in the RED phase". Is the requirement stated for both outcomes of that determination (a cell is added, or "defensive" is recorded), together with which documents change in each case? [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-011, contract "Changes from 1.14", tasks T012]
- [ ] CHK026 Does the nested cache state D10's "an answer that needs no slices is served uncached when its push fails" in the same terms as the flat cache, including the case where a failed push reuses an earlier row's table? [Consistency, research D10]
- [ ] CHK027 Is it stated that the kept span wrapper `group_slices()` still drops the status? Is it also stated that its callers keep a silent empty answer on failure, both `InboundFrameDispositionsQ33.GroupSlicesAtHeadroomExhaustion` and any production caller? [Gap, research D3, research "Census"]
