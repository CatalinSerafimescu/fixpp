# Contract — Concern B: QuickFIX group-gating parity

**Surface**: the per-group required-member store (runtime `table_view` + loaders) and the typed `validate_<Msg>` codegen tier. No public C-ABI/config surface change.

## Behavioral contract

On the strict-validation path, for a direct member declared `required='Y'` inside a repeating group:

The gating rule (decided at design time, D-3): a direct member is required iff `required=="Y" && groupRequired`, where `groupRequired` is the **immediate enclosing group's own** `required=` — NOT an AND across ancestor groups (QuickFIX `addXMLGroup`, `groupRequired` reset one level per recursion).

1. **Optional group → not required per-instance.** If the member's immediate enclosing group is **optional**, a present-but-incomplete instance (member omitted) MUST be **accepted** — matching QuickFIX.
2. **Required group → still required per-instance.** If the immediate enclosing group is **required**, a present-but-incomplete instance MUST still be **rejected** (`wire_required_field_missing`, ref_tag = first missing member).
3. **Two-tier agreement.** The runtime validator verdict and the generated typed `validate_<Msg>` verdict MUST be identical at every affected group context (FR-007). Typed agreement is delivered by forking the group-plan interning identity on enclosing-group-required-ness (E-4), so a structural group used both required and optional emits distinct `required_checks` per usage.

## Census contract (non-circular, exact-set both directions)

Across every (dictionary, message, group-context):

- **Oracle side** = a raw-XML walk applying QuickFIX's group-gating rule (the reworked `required_scope_oracle.hpp`, gating on the **immediate enclosing group's own `required=`**, computed independently from the raw XML), independent of the loaded tables and of the parity golden. The parity golden **corroborates** this rule; it does not define it (keeps the census non-circular).
- **Actual side** = the loaded `table_view` per-group required-member store (what `consume_group` probes).
- **Assertion**: `oracle == actual` **exact-set, both directions**, for all in-scope dictionaries. The 24 previously-divergent contexts (from #205/PR#204 Gate B) collapse to **0**.
- Recount the census baselines that shrink: RC5 max-required-member count (was 6), total per-group contexts.

## Parity golden contract

- A required-set golden generated by **quickfix-cpp 1.16.0** (offline; not linked in CI) over the shared dictionaries, asserted exact against the loaded per-group required sets. fixpp ⊇/⊆ QuickFIX both hold (exact) at the 24 contexts, not merely superset.
- **Prerequisite (N2)**: `reference-engines/quickfix-cpp` must be cloned and built before the golden can be regenerated — it is gitignored / absent on a fresh tree ([[project_reference_engines_setup]]). A `/tasks` + `/verify` prerequisite; without it the parity oracle silently skips.

## Scope

- **Typed tier** (two-tier agreement pin): any *typed* version with an optional group carrying `required='Y'` members — **v44, v50sp2, vlatest** (typed goldens regenerated).
- **Runtime tier** (census + parity golden): all in-scope dictionaries. **N3**: the 24 previously-divergent contexts must be enumerated by `(version, message, group)` at `/tasks`; any context in a runtime-only version (no typed builder tier — e.g. v42 per L-077-1) has **no typed tier to compare** and is pinned by the census + parity golden alone (explicitly runtime-only-by-scope).
- FIX42 INT-group carve-out (#196/L-066-1) out of scope.
- Read/reify goldens unchanged (required-ness does not affect read membership).
