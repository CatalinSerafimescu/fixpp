- [ ] CHK017 Is a peer that never sends its readback (counterparty crash, readback writer absent in a stale binary, as R-12 records for the local QuickFIX-cpp build) defined as a failure by the "exactly one comparison row" condition, and is that link stated so a skipped or silent cell cannot read as a pass?
