## Ambiguities & Conflicts

- [x] CHK037 Does FR-031 "cancellation_type::total MUST surface as the matching transport_*_cancelled variant (no thrown exception across the public surface)" leave room for ASIO's `operation_aborted` to be thrown INTERNALLY inside the awaitable before being mapped? [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-031] — PASS: FR-031 says "no thrown exception across the public surface"; the internal `operation_aborted` exception inside the coroutine body is caught and mapped to the named variant before returning through `expected_t`. This is the standard ASIO+coroutine pattern (try/catch `operation_aborted` → return unexpected). FR-031 is unambiguous: it constrains the PUBLIC surface (what callers of `async_read_some` etc. see), not the internal exception path. No ambiguity.
- [x] CHK038 Is the relationship between FR-007's "API-level exclusivity" and FR-039's "depth-1 queue per `[arch §5.8]`" clear — does the depth-1 queue suppress the exclusivity check or stack with it? [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-007 §FR-039] — PASS: FR-039 says "outbound writes MUST serialise on the session strand by API construction (depth-1 queue per `[arch §5.8]`)"; FR-007 is the API-level exclusivity on the Transport. These operate at different layers: the strand is the session-level depth-1 queue (FSM side); the Transport exclusivity is a per-instance Transport contract. They stack: the strand prevents two coroutines from issuing `async_write` concurrently (depth-1 queue), and the Transport exclusivity is a defence-in-depth on the Transport instance. FR-039's note "There MUST be NO transport-internal write queue" clarifies the Transport has no internal queue — the strand IS the queue. No ambiguity.
- [x] CHK039 Is the verbiage "MUST follow the fresh-Transport-per-attempt pattern" (FR-022) consistent with FR-005's "cancel() MUST NOT close the socket — the FSM may retry a cancelled async_connect"? Does "retry" here mean retry on the SAME Transport or mint a fresh one? [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-005 §FR-022] — PASS: FR-005 says "the FSM may retry a cancelled `async_connect`"; FR-022 says "MUST follow the fresh-Transport-per-attempt pattern". These are consistent: `cancel()` signals the awaitable to abort without destroying the Transport (so the FSM can still call `close()` cleanly), BUT the fresh-per-attempt pattern means the FSM then destroys the Transport and mints a fresh one for the next attempt. "Retry" in FR-005 means "the FSM decides whether to retry" — it doesn't mandate retrying on the same Transport instance. FR-028 reconciliation paragraph makes this explicit.
