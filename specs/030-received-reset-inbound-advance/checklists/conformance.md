- [x] CHK019 Is the FR-010 fault-injection witness specified so it actually falsifies the soundness claim (persistent reset-fail → Disconnect AND persist-to-2 not reached)? [Measurability, Spec §Discriminating Witness / §SC-002] — PASS: the witnesses (T008/T014) seed the store to N=37 (a last-good durable value > 1) and assert (i) Disconnected + `reset_call_count()==1`, AND (ii) `store==37` (persist-to-2 not reached — store retains its last-good value). A trivially-seeded (N=1) witness cannot distinguish the fatal path from any other path that ends with store==1. **NOTE (Gate-B RC#1):** the previous phrasing "no `store > manager` ever observable" in spec FR-010 / SC-002 has been narrowed — a transient in-memory `store=N > manager=1` on the fatal teardown path is harmless (lower-bound-preserving); the guarantee is that persist-to-2 never runs after a failed reset (store never advanced past its last-good durable value). The witness asserts `store==N` (last-good retained), which is the direct evidence for that guarantee.
- [x] CHK020 Is SC-001 (the live close-out) measurable (session reaches Active, zero ResendRequest, seq-2 accepted, vs BOTH QFcpp and QFJ)? [Measurability, Spec §SC-001] — PASS: SC-001 states "Re-running the live acceptor interop cell against **both** QuickFIX-cpp and QuickFIX-J, after a peer `141=Y` reset, results in the session reaching Active with **zero** `ResendRequest` emitted by fixpp." Three measurable outcomes: (a) session state = Active, (b) zero ResendRequest emitted, (c) vs both QFcpp and QFJ. tasks.md T028 adds "peer's seq-2 message accepted" as a fourth clause.
