## Ledger Delta — Consistency & Measurability

- [ ] CHK009 FR-016 is tagged *(C-ABI)*, and the spec's own rule says C-ABI requirements are governed by `[const §X.7]` while C++ ones are not. Yet one of its four required rows is the C++-track handle refusal, and another spans both surfaces. Is the track labelling of FR-016 consistent with that rule? [Consistency, FR-016]
- [ ] CHK010 FR-016 requires *"no limitation row"*. The version contract and the task set also require the two out-of-scope residuals (the probe-cap degradation and RefMsgType(372) at the inbound-fed reject builders) to be *"written down"*, or they become Gate B defects. Does the spec state **where** they are recorded and in **what row class**, so the two requirements cannot conflict? [Conflict, FR-016, Spec §Explicitly out of scope]
- [ ] CHK011 Are the four rows' mandatory contents specified precisely enough to accept or reject a drafted row? That means the guard's "true width" as the two named refused-but-safe classes, "exactly what is not covered" for the C++ row, and the abort limb's "trigger set not enumerated" statement. [Measurability, FR-016]
