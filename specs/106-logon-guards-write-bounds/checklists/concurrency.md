## The connection bound: spawn, executor and lifetime (data-model E-3, research D3)

- [ ] CHK001 Is "at most once per connection" stated consistently? Plan "Technical Context" (Constraints) says "One `co_spawn` per connection … and none per write or emit". Data-model E-3 and research D3 add the respawn at `drive_reconnect`'s failed-drain return, so the rule becomes at most one running at a time. Does the plan's constraint carry that exception? [Consistency, Plan "Technical Context", Data-model E-3, Research D3]
- [ ] CHK002 Is the bound's executor stated for each spawner? When the first live write with the limit on spawns it, that write's coroutine can be an application `Session::send` on a direct driver. Is it stated that every spawner runs on the session's serialised domain, so that the bound's members stay strand-confined (XI §4)? [Clarity, Data-model E-3, Research D3, Plan "Constitution Check" (XI §4)]
- [ ] CHK003 Is a single writer stated for every new member, with no suspension between its test and its set? The members are `emit_in_flight_`, the write members, `ending_claim_` and `connection_bound_`. D11 says this for `claim_ending_`. Is it said for `live_write_serialized_` on a direct Session too? [Completeness, Data-model E-3, Data-model E-10]
- [ ] CHK004 Is the bound's lifetime stated against the Session and the io_context? Is it stated that `~Session`'s counter assertion is Debug-only, and that the release-build guarantee rests on the existing precondition (`close()` before destruction), as 105 stated for its own frames? [Completeness, Data-model E-3 joiner table, Research D3]
- [ ] CHK005 Is it stated what happens when the respawn's spawn is refused? Its guard is: `conn_gen_` unchanged, not `closing_or_stopped_()`, a write or emit in flight, `connection_bound_.running` false. "Fails closed as C-4's spawn failure" does not say whether that is the loop-start form or the live-write form. [Clarity, Data-model E-3 "Respawn after a failed drain"]
- [ ] CHK006 Is the statement that the respawn's `running` term "is defensive and has no cell" given as a structural observation with its re-derivation, rather than as a result? The re-derivation is that, on one strand, the old bound's exit runs during step 3's park. [Measurability, Data-model E-3, Plan "Deferred to tasks.md"]

## The wait primitive and the timer slot (#715, ruling R2)

- [ ] CHK007 Is it stated that the shipping-clock test (a `dynamic_cast` to `system_clock_source` at the spawn) is evaluated once per spawn? Is it stated whether `effective_clock_` can change during a connection? [Clarity, Research D3 "The primitive", Data-model E-3 member `shipping`]
- [ ] CHK008 Is plan Constraints' "no new call of `Clock::sleep_until` on the shipping clock beside another sleeper of the Session" consistent with the held cap with none pending, which adds an `await_deadline` at the loop's replay-gate wait? Is the claim "replaces the loop's own sleep, so it adds no concurrent sleeper" given its re-derivation (quickstart §1 "Sleepers")? [Consistency, Plan "Technical Context", Data-model E-3 "The held cap", Research D3 "#715 stays out of scope"]
- [ ] CHK009 On a non-shipping clock the bound waits through `await_deadline(*effective_clock_, …)` beside the liveness loop's own sleep. Is it stated whether a user `Clock` (`clock_override` or `EngineConfig::clock`) with a per-session slot lets the bound's wait reset the loop's sleep, the #715 hazard M8 shows on the slot path? `L-106-e` discloses only allocation. Is that hazard disclosed or excluded? [Coverage, Gap, Research D3 "The condition, disclosed", Contract C-8 `L-106-e`]
- [ ] CHK010 Is the bound's own-slot claim stated with each teardown's wake? The claim is that no other sleeper arms its timer, `Clock::cancel_sleeps()` does not reach it, and every teardown wakes it. Does data-model E-3's "Wakers" list equal research D3's "Its wakes"? [Consistency, Data-model E-3 "Wakers", Research D3]

## Wakes, re-tests and the transition rows (data-model E-3)

- [ ] CHK011 Is the order and totality of the transition rows stated? The rows are binding in order (N-7). Is it stated which row acts when an exit term and an action row both hold at one re-test, for example `closing_or_stopped_()` with the write expiry due? Is the exit row stated to precede every action row? [Completeness, Data-model E-3 transition table]
- [ ] CHK012 Is "due at one re-test, the give-up claims" (R9) defined in terms each wait branch can decide? On the mock clock, `step_to` sets the time before it completes the due waiters. On the shipping clock, two instants falling within one timer completion is a matter of scheduling. [Clarity, Spec Clarifications R9, Data-model E-3]
- [ ] CHK013 Is the rule for an inbound frame that moves L (and so G) later while the bound is armed on the old G stated as a requirement a cell can check? The current text says the bound "sees the move at its next wake, at the old G at the latest, and re-arms". The rule would be: a give-up is never decided before the current G. [Measurability, Research D3 "The re-test", Data-model E-4]
- [ ] CHK014 Is the rule "a write wakes the bound unless it is already armed on an earlier write's deadline" stated for the case where the earlier write has returned? There, the bound finds the newer epoch at its deadline. Is the newer write's own deadline then guaranteed as its start + W, never the old deadline + W? [Clarity, Research D5 "Mechanism", Data-model E-3 "Wakers"]
- [ ] CHK015 Is `write_epoch_` (a `std::uint32_t`) specified against wrap? Is it stated that a wrap within one connection cannot make the bound's same-epoch test, or `write_cut_epoch_`, match a different write? [Edge Case, Gap, Data-model E-3 members]
- [ ] CHK016 Are the RAII clears of `emit_in_flight_` and `write_in_flight_` on an exception or a `catch` return required to wake the bound? Is the tasks-time re-derivation (every such return reaches the edge) stated as an obligation, with an outcome for a return that does not reach it? [Completeness, Data-model E-5, Plan "Deferred to tasks.md"]

- [ ] CHK020 Is "taken once, at the decision" for the Logouts written under their decider's claim (`run_logout_phase1`, and `inbound_logout_leaf_` for kind `r1`) stated with a census of every other ending-Logout claim site? - [ ] CHK021 Is it stated that 105's ending limit is not a claimant, and that its cut of a claimant's write records nothing? Is that consistent with C-4.6's last sentence and with data-model E-3's failure row ("with the claim taken, nothing")? [Consistency, Research D11, Contract C-4.6, Data-model E-3]
- [ ] CHK022 Is the one change for a lone decider (N-12) stated as a behaviour change, with its reachability ruled? With the claim refused, the loop's give-up block now records nothing and exits. - [ ] CHK023 Is the census of unconditional `Disconnected` writes stated as an obligation with a closed outcome per hit (converted, or named unreachable)? Is its tie to INV-106-2 stated, so that a `Disconnected` site added later has to go through `record_disconnected_unless_already_()`? [Completeness, Research D11, Data-model E-10 INV-106-2, Quickstart §4 M13]
