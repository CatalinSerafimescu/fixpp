## The `drive_reconnect` operation contract

- [ ] CHK015 FR-006 says the emit wait "is bounded by `logout_disconnect_timeout_ms`, after which the old transport is force-closed". Research and B-103-9 disclose an unbounded residual: a wedged store call (B-598-1). Does FR-006 state that exception, so "bounded" is not read as total? [Conflict, Spec §FR-006, Research D1 step 3 "Bound", Research "B&L delta" B-103-9]
- [ ] CHK016 Are the operation's endings stated in one place, each with the state it leaves?
  - refused, closed: `session_already_closed`;
  - fenced: `session_already_open`;
  - single flight: `session_already_open`;
  - a cancelled drain: `dispatch_aborted`;
  - a refused install: `session_already_closed`;
  - a cancellation in backoff, connect or handshake: fixpp#585's existing result.

  [Completeness, Spec §FR-006, Data-model "Reconnect drain", Contract session-wire-endings E4]
- [ ] CHK017 Is the order of single flight and the closed test stated the same way everywhere? Research D1 (step 0b, single flight, then step 1, then step 1a) and data-model ("single flight and the callback post; refuse if closing …; then the reader fence") test single flight first. Contract E4 (closing/closed/stopped, then the fence, then "one at a time") and FR-006's sentence order test it last. The order decides which code a second call on a closing Session returns: `session_already_open` under D1's order, `session_already_closed` under E4's. [Conflict, Contract session-wire-endings E4, Spec §FR-006, Research D1 steps 0b, 1 and 1a, Data-model "Reconnect drain"]
- [ ] CHK018 Is the reader fence's predicate stated identically in FR-006, data-model, E4 and B-103-9? The predicate is: an acceptor, or an `engine_managed` initiator with a transport installed. The disclosed false positive (a standalone initiator that sets `engine_managed`) should appear in each. [Consistency, Spec §FR-006, Data-model "Reconnect drain", Contract session-wire-endings E4, Research "B&L delta" B-103-9]
- [ ] CHK019 The standalone reader precondition is documented and not detected. Is the consequence of breaking it stated, that is, what happens to a reader still on the old `live_transport()`? The header doc needs it to describe the hazard. [Gap, Spec §FR-006, Research D1 step 1a]
- [ ] CHK020 Quiescence has two phases: first the emits and the inbound chain, then the detached coroutines after `root_cancel_.emit(total)`. Is the reason that the emit retires exactly the old liveness loop stated (the root slot has a single client)? Is it stated that `drive_reconnect` itself must not be bound to that slot? Both should be in T153's header-doc requirement, not only in research. [Completeness, Research D1 step 3 "The root slot's single-client invariant", Tasks T153, T134]
- [ ] CHK021 The drain ruling says no frame "numbered or claimed" on the old connection is written on the new one. Is "claimed" defined: the `acking` phase only, or also any Logout that holds the owner flag? The invariant should name every holder the drain must wait for. [Clarity, Spec §Clarifications (Gate A round 1, drain ruling), Data-model "Reconnect drain" (Invariant)]
- [ ] CHK022 A call from a callback runs "after one post, as `close()` does". Is it stated what the post achieves: step 1's test runs after it, and `onLogout` does not run nested in the callback? Is naming the 563 §2.1a supersession in the header comment stated as a requirement? [Completeness, Research D1 step 0b, Tasks T153]
- [ ] CHK023 `drive_reconnect` from Active now fires `onLogout`. It can also return `session_already_closed`, `dispatch_aborted` or `session_already_open` without reconnecting (R24(d)). That is a change to public C++ behaviour. Is it classified, BREAKING for C++ or not, as 097's `which()` change and 098's removed field were? Is the classification stated in the spec, B-103-9 and T181's PR-body draft? [Gap, Research "Pass-2 amendments" R24(d), Research "B&L delta" B-103-9, Tasks T181]
- [ ] CHK024 The reconnect leaves four latches set: `onLogon_fired_`, `onLogout_fired_`, `reached_active_` and `logout_seen_`. Is it stated which callbacks a standalone caller therefore does not receive on the new connection? It should be in the header-doc requirement and in B-103-9. [Coverage, Research D1 step 5, Research "B&L delta" B-103-9]
