## Text(58) (FR-003, research D3)

- [ ] CHK007 FR-003 says every session Reject the inbound validation gate sends "MUST carry Text(58)". D3 gives the map's fail-closed default (373=3) an empty text. Does the spec state that exception, with D3's claim that 3 is unreachable on the in-contract path, so that FR-003's "every" can be checked? [Conflict, Spec §FR-003, research D3]
- [ ] CHK008 D3 lists the reasons that get a text (0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 11, 13, 14). That list is a result. Is there a recipe that derives it from `wire_error_to_session_reject_reason`'s range plus the gate's 099 refusals? Without one, a reason the map can return and the list omits, such as a group-structure result, would ship with no text unnoticed. [Measurability, research D3, research "Survey"]
- [ ] CHK009 "An empty view otherwise" (D3): is it stated which gate paths, if any, produce a reason outside the list, and that such a Reject is sent with no 58 rather than refused? [Edge Case, research D3, data-model "Reason text"]
- [ ] CHK010 FR-003 keeps the 092 disposer's own text. A malformed tag from the disposer and an undefined tag from the gate both answer 373=0, and their 58 strings may now differ. Is that stated as intended? [Consistency, Spec §FR-003, Spec "Out of Scope"]
- [ ] CHK011 The spec, the contract and data-model quote the texts. Is it stated that the authority is QuickFIX C++ `Values.h` at the cited commit, transcribed by tasks T004, and that the quoted strings are informative, so a quoting slip in a document cannot become a pinned literal? [Clarity, Spec §US2, tasks T004]
- [ ] CHK012 D3 discloses two QuickFIX C++ differences: the form below FIX.4.2, and the omission of 373 above 11 on FIX.4.2. On FIX.4.2, 13 and 14 are therefore sent with a 373 that QuickFIX C++ leaves out. Does the contract's validation-gate table (or its notes) say so, so a FIX.4.2 counterparty's diff is expected? [Completeness, research D3, contract "Validating session (FIXT or not), validation gate"]
- [ ] CHK013 Both a header-order fault and a past-header repeated 35 answer 14. Is it stated that the text follows the reason alone, so the two Rejects carry the same 58 and a reader of 58 cannot tell them apart? [Clarity, Spec §FR-003, contract "Repeated MsgType (a later 35 whose value differs from the first)"]

## The 372 echo and the outbound ceiling (FR-007, research D7)

- [ ] CHK016 For a BMR that is not sent, FR-007 (b) states the inbound frame's disposition: consumed as today, the session in its state. FR-007 (a), the Reject that is not sent, states only "no callback, no outbound number, the record". Is the inbound frame's disposition (consumed, NextNumIn, state) stated for (a) in the spec? - [ ] CHK017 A Reject above the ceiling is sent without 372 and writes no log record: records come only from the not-sent endings. Is that silence stated as intended, given that losing 372 departs from QuickFIX? [Gap, Spec §FR-007, research D7 "Outbound-ceiling ending"]
