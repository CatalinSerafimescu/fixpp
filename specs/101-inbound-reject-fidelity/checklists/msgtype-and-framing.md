### Escalation record (CHK006, CHK008, CHK010; resolved by the owner ruling above, option (b))
- CHK006, CHK008, CHK010 — the scan's hooks (the route's, whole frame) versus D5's walk (S's for the header, the route's from the stopping field).
  - **Shipped dictionaries: unreachable.** A standard pair is answered by the standard table whatever the hooks
    (`dict_hooks::data_tag_for_length`). No vendored file declares a header pair outside it
    (`W17Overload.VendoredFileDeclaresNoHeaderPair`, and re-measured at the audit by a scan of every vendored file's
    header, its known positive a seeded FIXT11 header pair). FIXT11 declares no non-standard DATA field at all, and the
    only non-standard pairs (FIX50SP2's and FIX Latest's body pairs) are read with the route's hooks by both walks.
  - **Custom, "S declares a header pair the route does not": unreachable.** `fixt_route_set::make` registers each
    listed S's `dict::header_length_pairs` into every other dictionary's table, S winning a collision (099 R-20,
    `W17ExtraPairs.SPairWinsACollisionOnThatTable`). Every FIXT session's dictionary is listed (`Engine::start`;
    `Session::open()` for a direct session), and `bind_fixt_routes_` picks the `{d, S}` entries.
  - **Custom residual: reachable.** `header_length_pairs` tests S's own `header_tags_`, while the walk classifies by
    S's header *union* (`is_header_tag` ∪ `is_fixt_standard_header_tag`). Take an S that pairs a non-standard Length
    on a FIXT-standard header tag it leaves out of its own header. That pair is not merged into the routes, so the
    route-hooked scan splits its Data and an embedded `SOH 35=X` reads as a later 35, setting `msg_type_conflict`.
    The walk, reading that field with S's hooks, counts the Data and sees no later 35. The ending is the default
    clause, 373=14, for a conformant frame in LogonReceived/Active, and a disconnect in an establishment arm (the
    parser, validator and view also split by the route, so the frame has two MsgTypes from the route's view).
    The reverse direction (the route pairs a header-union tag S does not) cannot trigger a refusal, because the walk
    runs only on a flagged conflict, but it can turn a real conflict's 13 into 14.
  - **Consequence for the witnesses (CHK010).** On route-set-built routes the walk can fault only where it counts a
    pair the route's scan does not, which is the residual above; anywhere else the route-hooked scan faults first and
    the 092 disposer takes the frame. So W101-29 (c) cannot reach D5's fault rule through a real Session, and M101-24
    would survive (a witness defect that stops T042's batch), unless the residual is built or the cell moves to a unit
    instrument with disagreeing hooks.
  - **Fixes, each keeping one ending (the owner chose (b), 2026-11-07):** (a) widen `dict::header_length_pairs`'s header test to S's header
    union, a one-predicate change in 099's merge rule that leaves D5 intact and closes the direction that misfires;
    (b) read the walk with the route's hooks throughout ("a route is one table", R-20), so scan and walk split alike
    by construction in both directions, at the cost of D5's S-for-the-header rule, M101-2/M101-3 and W101-29's
    subject; (c) keep D5 and disclose the residual as a B-row, with W101-29 (c)/M101-24 re-homed to a reachable
    instrument.
