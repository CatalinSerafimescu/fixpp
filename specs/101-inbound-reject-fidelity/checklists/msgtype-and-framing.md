- [ ] CHK005 The resend replay and the outbound store masking controls rest on "stored outbound frames have one MsgType" (spec "Edge Cases"). ## Two walks, two sets of hooks (research D4, research D5)

- [ ] CHK006 `msg_type_conflict` comes from the header scan alone. The inbound arms' scan calls in `session.cpp` read the whole frame with the selected route's hooks (`session_hooks(*route.entry.owner)`). D5's walk reads the header with S's hooks and the rest with the route's, and D5 says "nothing pins that they agree". Is the ending stated for each kind of disagreement? (i) The scan flags a conflict the walk cannot see. Example: S declares a header Length/Data pair the route does not, and its Data embeds `SOH 35=`, which the route's hooks split. In Active the default clause then answers 14 for a frame whose only "later 35" is inside a Data value; in an establishment arm, if a route other than S can be selected there, the frame would be disconnected (reachability is CHK007's question). (ii) The walk sees a later 35 the scan did not flag, so no refusal runs. [Gap, Conflict, research D5, research D4]
- [ ] CHK007 Can CHK006's disagreement happen on a shipped pairing (FIXT11 with a FIX50SP2 or FIX Latest route)? Is the set of Length/Data pairs one side declares and the other does not stated, or given by a recipe? [Coverage, research D5]
- [ ] CHK008 Is it stated as a requirement that the scan's conflict and D5's "any later 35" agree on fault-free frames, with the hooks each uses named? Tasks T050's fuzz arm checks the scan against `entries()`, but no requirement says which hooks `entries()` is built with. [Measurability, tasks T050, research D5]
